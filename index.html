    Color tags

    Wrap words in tags to color them:

    • <gold>Last Whisper</gold> for items and keywords
    • <red>Draven</red> for enemies, <blue>Leona</blue> for allies
    • <ping>…</ping> for smart ping text, <info>…</info> for statuses like " - Ready"
    • <green> and <cyan> for stat values, <yellow> for bounty gold
    • <#ff66cc>anything</> for any hex color
